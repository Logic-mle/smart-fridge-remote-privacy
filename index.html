Privacy Policy for Smart Fridge Remote

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Smart Fridge Remote is provided by Guangdong Lixing. It is an app for finding and controlling compatible portable refrigerators over Bluetooth. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information the app accesses

When you choose to scan for a refrigerator, the app uses your device's Bluetooth permissions to discover nearby devices. It reads nearby Bluetooth advertisements and identifiers, including a device name, Bluetooth address or advertised MAC address, so it can show and connect to compatible refrigerators. Once connected, it reads refrigerator status such as temperatures, battery voltage, operating mode, and fault codes, and sends the control commands you select.

On some Android versions, Bluetooth scanning also requires location permission. The app requests location permission for Bluetooth discovery only. It does not read your GPS coordinates or use your location for tracking, advertising, or analytics. You can use the in-app demo without connecting to a physical refrigerator.

Collection, sharing, and use

The current app has no user account, advertising, analytics, or cloud service. It does not send your location, Bluetooth identifiers, or refrigerator status to the developer or to a third-party server. Bluetooth information and refrigerator status are processed on your device to show and control the refrigerator. Control commands are sent directly to the connected refrigerator over Bluetooth.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The app does not create a cloud profile or intentionally store scan results or refrigerator status in persistent app storage. It may keep this information in memory while the app is running; it is cleared when the app process ends. You can stop scanning, disconnect the refrigerator, revoke Bluetooth or location permission in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android may manage its own permission and Bluetooth records separately.

Security

The app limits use of Bluetooth data to local device discovery and control. It does not operate a server that receives this data. Bluetooth communication with a refrigerator depends on the device's own Bluetooth implementation; do not assume that the link is end-to-end encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, we will update this page and the date above before or when the changes take effect.
